Academy highlight

Learning doesn’t stop at the classroom door.

From hands-on projects to Cambodia Robotics Olympiad participation, our learners take their ideas into the wider robotics community.

By RobotYOUNG Academy · Published 14 October 2026

Ideas become something you can build

Robotics brings science, technology, engineering and mathematics together. At RobotYOUNG Academy, learners explore these subjects through hands-on projects, turning ideas into builds they can test and improve.

A wider community of makers

Learning reaches beyond classroom activities through student projects and Cambodia Robotics Olympiad participation. Explore the academy’s project page for a closer look at what young makers are working on.
